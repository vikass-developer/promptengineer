# HR policy (sample)

- Full-time employees get 22 days of PTO per year. Unused PTO up to 5 days carries over to next year.
- PTO requests longer than 3 consecutive days need manager approval 2 weeks in advance.
- Remote work: up to 3 days per week; Tuesdays are in-office for all teams.
- Home-office stipend: 400 EUR once, plus 30 EUR per month for internet.
